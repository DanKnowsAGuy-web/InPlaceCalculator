Methodology and pending confirmations

Asphalt thickness: AASHTO 1993 Guide Table 2-5 layer coefficients — a2 interpolated between 0.14 (CBR 80%) and 0.18 (CBR 100%) for conventional base; a2 = 0.20 for In-Place BaseGrade at 125% CBR. Required SN divided by a1 = 0.44 after base contribution subtracted.

Aggregate tonnage: project volume × 110 lbs/CF compacted density (RS Means standard). Asphalt tonnage: volume × 145 lbs/CF.

Pending field confirmation: (1) In-Place treatment pass labor — milling machine rate and production rate require field confirmation.
