Methodology and pending confirmations

Asphalt thickness: AASHTO 1993 layered SN — a2 interpolated between 0.14 (CBR 80%) and 0.18 (CBR 100%) for conventional aggregate base, plus a3 = 0.11 for conventional sub-base where present; a2 = 0.20 for the In-Place treated base (the conservative bottom of the published stabilized-base range 0.20–0.27, Iowa SUDAS 5J-3 / FHWA-SA-98-042 — final value requires a project mix design). Remaining SN divided by a1 = 0.44.

Aggregate tonnage: layer volume × 110 lbs/CF compacted density (RS Means standard). Asphalt tonnage: volume × 145 lbs/CF. Hauled volumes: bank volume × swell factor (default 1.25). Overhead and profit are % of direct cost; tax applies to the marked-up subtotal; the direct-cost subtotal is always shown separately.

Required SN: AASHTO 1993 flexible design equation solved from design ESALs, subgrade Mr (2555 × CBR^0.64, NCHRP 1-37A), reliability by road class (local 80% / collector 85% / arterial 90% / urban arterial 95%), S0 = 0.45, ΔPSI = 4.2 − 2.25. Minimum asphalt thickness enforced per the AASHTO minimum-thickness table (1.0″ at ≤50k ESALs up to 4.0″ above 7M); a surface treatment may replace asphalt only at ≤50k design ESALs, and chip seal carries zero structural credit.

Lifecycle: constant dollars discounted to NPV at the real discount rate (FHWA-SA-98-079 / OMB A-94); rehab schedule per alternative; salvage = remaining service life of the last rehab, prorated and discounted; chip-seal reseal cycle charged to the In-Place side when selected.

Deliberately excluded (with rationale): work-zone user delay costs — negligible on low-volume roads and their exclusion is conservative toward In-Place, which has fewer construction days (FHWA-SA-98-079 permits exclusion with justification); vehicle-operating-cost effects of surface condition — HDM-4 territory, not calibrated for this tool; drainage, right-of-way, and structures — equal under either alternative in a same-corridor comparison, so they cancel (Caltrans convention); mechanistic-empirical design (Pavement ME) — calibrated for ≥4″ asphalt sections, not thin/low-volume roads.

Pending confirmation: (1) In-Place treatment-pass labor — confirm with your operator's milling rate ÷ production. (2) Conventional duration defaults — field experience may tighten them. (3) In-Place a2 structural basis — pending 7-day UCS mix-design verification. (4) Product price — 2017 list price; confirm current/volume pricing. (5) The base/subgrade maintenance share — planning placeholder; the often-quoted 78% figure is under source verification (NCHRP 602) and is not used as a default.
